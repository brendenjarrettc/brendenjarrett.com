GRANDPA'S CLOTHES EP

10-9-2025

Grandpa's Clothes is a touch with nostalgia. The sound slowly degrades as the EP goes on. It is a four track EP and each represents to me a significant part of one's life that is long lived and whether it is fighting with the pains of the past or moving forward with optimism. The sound is overall acoustic instruments coupled with synthesizers. I wanted to clash both worlds on this project.

Tracklist:

  1. All Those Words
  2. Dose
  3. Woulnd
  4. Grandpa's Clothes

The artwork was created by splicing some images of clothing and post processing using a snippet of a noise CHOP in touchdesigner and finalizing in Adobe Photoshop.
