EFFERVESCENT

December 2, 2022

Effervescent is a sporadic, bodacious, and bubbly glass of red wine in electronica. My first release on streaming services. This album is broken down into four major segments of sound. Beats, Ambience, Piano, and Guitar.

The artwork was inspired from a printing malfunction. The CMYK smudged and smeared into a unique hue. I took that as inspiration and mixed an Effervescent amount of typography to sit on a powerline. If you look closely, you can see the magic falling.
