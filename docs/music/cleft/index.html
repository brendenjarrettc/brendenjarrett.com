CLEFT

6-23-2023

Cleft means divide. The theme of the music has an astronomical focus. Large sweeping reverbs with some experimental takes on synthesizers.

This image was taken of a small town skyline one night when looking at the stars. The noise/grain in the bottom left of the image reminds me of a galaxy horizon.
